The problem

Every payment leaves a bill behind. Almost nobody keeps it.

What you get after paying is a thermal slip that fades in weeks, an email you'll never find again, or nothing at all. That bill is the only record of what you actually bought — and without it, money quietly leaks away.

WHAT A LOST BILL COSTS YOUItemised · Tax invoice not available
01

Reimbursements left unclaimed

No bill, no claim. Finance won't pay for a slip you lost in a cab.

02

Warranties you can't prove

The laptop dies in month eleven. The invoice is buried in last year's inbox.

03

Spending with zero context

Forty UPI entries that say "paid to merchant". None of them say what you bought.

04

Paper that doesn't go away

An estimated 93% of paper receipts are coated with BPA or BPS, which makes them hard to recycle and harmful to handle.
